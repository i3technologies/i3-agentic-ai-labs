1 — Overview

EvalOS is i3 Technologies' AI-powered skills assessment platform. It enables organisations, training institutions, and professional bodies to design, administer, and automatically grade assessments — and gives learners instant AI feedback to accelerate their growth.

CapabilityDescription
Exams & AssessmentsMCQ, short answer, essay, and live coding questions with AI auto-grading
Study CoachAI tutor (Qwen 7B via LiteLLM) that explains wrong answers and builds study plans
AI InterviewSimulated mock interviews with behavioural and technical questions; AI feedback report generated
Coding LabMonaco editor sandbox — write, run, and submit code for AI review in multiple languages
LeaderboardReal-time ranking by department, cohort, or date range
Zuri Co-workerAlways-on chat assistant for platform navigation, exam tips, and study resources

2 — Getting Started

2.1 First Login

  1. Open your browser and navigate to https://evalos.i3technologies.co.ke.
  2. Click Sign In. You will be redirected to the i3 Single Sign-On portal (sso.i3technologies.co.ke).
  3. Enter your email address (e.g. yourname@i3technologies.co.ke) and the password provided by your administrator.
  4. Click Log In. On your first login you may be prompted to verify your email — check your inbox and click the confirmation link.
  5. You will be redirected back to the EvalOS dashboard.
NoteCredentials are managed centrally via Keycloak SSO. Your same username and password work across all i3 platform products.

2.2 Changing Your Password

  1. Click your avatar (top-right) → Account Settings.
  2. Select Password from the left menu.
  3. Enter your current password, then your new password twice, and click Save.

2.3 Profile Setup

  1. Click your avatar → Profile.
  2. Complete your department, job title, and profile photo.
  3. Click Save Changes. Your profile details appear on the leaderboard and exam submissions.

4 — Taking an Exam

4.1 Finding and Starting an Exam

  1. From the Dashboard, click Exams in the top navigation bar.
  2. Browse the list of available exams. Use the filter bar to search by topic or date.
  3. Click an exam card to open its detail page. Review the instructions, time limit, and question count.
  4. Click Start Exam. The timer starts immediately — do not navigate away.
WarningOnce started, an exam session cannot be paused. Ensure you have a stable internet connection and sufficient time before clicking Start Exam.

4.2 Question Types

TypeDescriptionHow to Answer
Multiple Choice (MCQ)Single correct answer from 4 optionsClick the radio button next to your answer
Multi-SelectOne or more correct answersTick all correct checkboxes
Short AnswerTyped response, 1–3 sentencesType in the text field; AI grades for key concepts
EssayLonger written responseUse the rich text editor; AI evaluates structure and content
CodingWrite code to pass test casesOpens an embedded Coding Lab editor — see Section 7

4.3 Navigating Questions

  • Use Previous / Next buttons or the question number grid on the left sidebar.
  • Flag a question for review by clicking the flag icon — flagged questions show in yellow in the sidebar.
  • Answered questions show a green dot; unanswered show grey.

4.4 Submitting and Viewing Results

  1. Click Submit Exam when done. A confirmation dialog lists any unanswered questions.
  2. Click Confirm Submit. Grading runs immediately.
  3. The Results page shows your total score, per-question breakdown, and AI-generated feedback for each wrong answer.
  4. Click Open Study Coach on any question to get a deeper explanation (see Section 5).
NoteResults for essay and short-answer questions may take up to 30 seconds while the AI grades your response. The page refreshes automatically.

5 — Study Coach

The Study Coach is an AI tutor powered by the Qwen 7B Instruct model running on-cluster via LiteLLM. It is context-aware — it knows which exam you took and which questions you got wrong.

5.1 Opening Study Coach

  1. After submitting an exam, on the Results page click Open Study Coach (top-right of any question card).
  2. Alternatively, go to My Attempts, find a past attempt, and click the book icon.
  3. The Study Coach panel opens alongside your exam summary. The AI has already read your attempt and is ready.

5.2 Sample Prompts

GoalSample Prompt
Understand a wrong answer"Explain why my answer to question 3 was wrong and what the correct reasoning is."
Build a study plan"Create a 2-week study plan for the topics I scored below 60% on."
Deeper concept explanation"Explain OSI Model Layer 4 in simple terms with a real-world analogy."
Practice questions"Give me 5 practice questions on subnetting similar to the ones I got wrong."
Summarise weak areas"Summarise my three weakest topics from this exam."
NoteThe Study Coach runs entirely on-cluster — no data leaves i3 infrastructure. Responses typically arrive within 5–15 seconds on CPU-only inference.

6 — AI Interview

The AI Interview feature simulates a professional job interview. It presents questions and evaluates your typed responses, then generates a structured feedback report.

6.1 Starting an Interview

  1. Click AI Interview in the top navigation or Dashboard quick links.
  2. Select an interview type: Behavioural, Technical, or Mixed.
  3. Choose the target role or topic area (e.g. "Software Engineer — Backend", "Data Analyst").
  4. Click Start Interview. Questions are presented one at a time.

6.2 Question Types

TypeExamples
Behavioural"Tell me about a time you resolved a conflict in your team."
Technical"Explain the difference between TCP and UDP. When would you use each?"
Situational"A critical production service goes down at 2 AM. Walk me through your response."
Competency"How do you prioritise tasks when you have multiple urgent deadlines?"

6.3 Viewing Your Feedback Report

After submitting your final answer the AI generates a structured report covering:

  • Overall Score — percentage rating across all responses
  • Communication Quality — clarity, structure, and conciseness
  • Technical Accuracy (technical questions only) — correctness and depth
  • STAR Method Usage (behavioural) — Situation, Task, Action, Result coverage
  • Improvement Tips — 3–5 specific, actionable suggestions per question
TipUse Export PDF on the report page to save your interview feedback for personal review or to share with a career coach.

7.3 Submitting Code for AI Review

  1. Once your code passes the visible test cases (green tick appears), click Submit for Review or press Ctrl+Shift+S.
  2. The AI analyses code quality, efficiency, edge-case handling, and style.
  3. Feedback appears in the right panel — inline comments are shown directly in the editor gutter.

8 — Leaderboard

The Leaderboard ranks all EvalOS users by weighted assessment performance. Use it to benchmark your progress against peers.

8.1 Scoring Weights

ComponentWeight
Exam average score60%
AI Interview score25%
Coding Lab submissions (rated)15%

8.2 Filters

  • Department — compare only within your business unit
  • Cohort / Batch — filter by training cohort or intake group
  • Date Range — view rankings for a specific period (e.g. last 30 days)
  • Exam / Topic — rank by performance on a specific exam or topic
NoteYour leaderboard entry is only visible to users in the same organisation. Administrators can control visibility in the admin panel.

9 — Zuri AI Co-worker

Zuri is the always-on AI assistant embedded in EvalOS. Unlike the Study Coach (which is tied to a specific exam attempt), Zuri answers general questions about the platform, learning resources, and study strategies at any time.

9.1 Opening Zuri

  1. Look for the chat bubble icon in the bottom-right corner of any EvalOS page.
  2. Click the bubble to open the Zuri chat panel.
  3. Type your question or pick from the suggested prompts shown on first open.

9.2 What Zuri Can Help With

Use CaseExample Question
Platform navigation"How do I find my past exam results?"
Exam tips"What's the best strategy for timed MCQ exams?"
Study resources"Recommend resources to learn Python for data analysis."
Understanding features"What is the Coding Lab and how do I use it?"
Career guidance"What certifications should I pursue for cloud architecture?"
