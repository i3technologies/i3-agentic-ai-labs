How to log in as Administrator:

  1. Open https://sso.i3technologies.co.ke/auth/admin/
  2. Select realm master from the dropdown (top-left)
  3. Enter username admin and password above → click Sign In

  5. Navigate to Users, Clients, Realm Settings as needed
