Replaces: watsonx.ai SaaS (unavailable under IBM Compute-only credits)  ·  Cluster: IBM ROKS 4.17, eu-de, 12 × bx2.4x16 (192 GB RAM total)  ·  Generated: 10 September 2026

Why This Change

IBM Cloud credits are Compute-only — they cover ROKS worker nodes, VPC, COS and ICR registry. They do not cover watsonx.ai SaaS (a separate paid service). The current LiteLLM config has two Tier-1 models (granite-3-8b-instruct and granite-vision-3-2-2b) pointing to eu-de.ml.cloud.ibm.com with placeholders for WATSONX_API_KEY and WATSONX_PROJECT_ID that will never be filled under the current credit structure.
Constraint: All AI inference must run inside the ROKS cluster on CPU-only workers (bx2.4x16 = 4 vCPU / 16 GB each). No GPU nodes available in eu-de. All models must be quantised (Q4/Q5 GGUF) to fit within CPU memory budgets. IBM Granite models can still be used — they are fully open-weight on HuggingFace and run perfectly through Ollama.
