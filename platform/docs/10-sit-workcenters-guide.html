Admin Guide

1. Platform Architecture & 4-Engine Model

SIT Digital Work-Centers is a multi-purpose campus platform deployed under the i3-sit Kubernetes namespace. It combines four independent service engines behind a single FastAPI backend, shared PostgreSQL database (sit_db), and Keycloak SSO.

1

TVET Education

Learner enrollment, course management, EvalOS exam bridge, SHA-256 anchored credential issuance, and multilingual AI tutor.

2

eCitizen Services

Digital government service delivery — citizens submit service cases that are routed to the appropriate department and tracked to resolution.

3

Professional Services Marketplace

Searchable directory of professional consultants with real-time WebRTC video room allocation on booking confirmation.

4

Co-working Membership

Physical and digital co-working space access with four subscription tiers: Daily, Monthly, Student, and Corporate.

3. Managing Learners

Learner Registration Requirements

Every learner must provide valid Kenyan identification. Under the Kenya Data Protection Act 2019, explicit consent (consent=true) is mandatory before any personal data is stored. The API will return 400 Bad Request if consent is omitted or false.

FieldTypeRequiredNotes
keycloak_substring (UUID)YesFrom Keycloak JWT sub claim — canonical identity
full_namestringYesLegal name as on ID
kenya_idstringYesNational ID or Alien Certificate number
phonestringYesKenyan format e.g. +254712345678
emailstringYesMust be valid email
wardstringYesSub-county ward — used for regional analytics
consentbooleanYesMust be true (Data Protection Act 2019)

  "phone": "+254712345678",
  "email": "amina.ochieng@example.com",
  "ward": "Westlands",
  "consent": true

  "keycloak_sub": "a1b2c3d4-e5f6-7890-abcd-ef1234567890",

Querying the learners Table

-- List all learners with enrollment count
SELECT l.id, l.full_name, l.email, l.ward, COUNT(e.id) AS enrollments
FROM learners l
LEFT JOIN enrollments e ON e.learner_id = l.id
GROUP BY l.id
ORDER BY l.created_at DESC
LIMIT 50;
Data Protection: The kenya_id and phone columns are considered personal data under the DPA 2019. Do not export or log these values outside approved systems. Learner data deletion requests must be honoured within 72 hours.

4. Managing Courses

Courses are the core TVET unit. The courses table stores all programme offerings. Course assets (syllabi, videos, reading materials) are stored in the sit-course-content SeaweedFS bucket and referenced by URL in the content_url column.

courses Table Schema

ColumnTypeDescription
idserial PKAuto-incrementing course ID
titlevarchar(255)Course title
descriptiontextFull course description
levelvarchar(50)e.g. Certificate I, Certificate III, Diploma
duration_weeksintegerPlanned duration in weeks
content_urltextSeaweedFS URL to course package
evalos_course_idvarchar(100)Corresponding EvalOS course identifier for exam bridging
activebooleanWhether course is open for enrollment
created_attimestamptzCreation timestamp

Enrollment links a learner to a course. After successful enrollment, the learner gains access to course content and becomes eligible to book an exam through the EvalOS bridge.

Enrollment API Flow

Content-Type: application/json

{
  "learner_id": 42,
  "course_id": 7

--- Response 201 ---
{

  "status": "active",

EvalOS Exam Bridge

When an exam booking is created, the SIT API passes the evalos_course_id and learner details to EvalOS. EvalOS returns a booking reference and scheduled date.

  "centre_code": "SIT-NBI-01"
}

--- Response 201 ---
{
  "id": 88,
  "enrollment_id": 201,
  "evalos_booking_ref": "EVL-2025-0045231",
  "scheduled_date": "2025-08-15",

Enrollment State Machine

[registered] → (POST /enrollments) → [active]
[active]     → (POST /exam-bookings) → [exam_booked]
[exam_booked] → (EvalOS webhook on pass) → [passed]
[passed]     → (POST /credentials) → [credentialed]

6. Credential Issuance

Credentials are issued after a learner passes their exam. The issuance process computes a SHA-256 hash of the credential payload, stores it in the credentials table, generates a QR token, and writes the record to the talent-api for cross-platform visibility.

Credential Issuance — API Example

POST https://api.sit.i3technologies.co.ke/api/v1/credentials
Authorization: Bearer <admin_access_token>
Content-Type: application/json

{
  "learner_id": 42,
  "course_id": 7,
  "exam_booking_id": 88,
  "grade": "PASS",
  "issue_date": "2025-08-20"
}

--- Response 201 ---
{
  "id": 15,
  "learner_id": 42,
  "course_id": 7,
  "qr_token": "SIT-CRED-a9f3e2c1b0d847f6",
  "cert_hash": "sha256:e3b0c44298fc1c149afbf4c8996fb92427ae41e4649b934ca495991b7852b855",
  "verify_url": "https://verify.sit.i3technologies.co.ke/api/v1/credentials/verify/SIT-CRED-a9f3e2c1b0d847f6",
  "issued_at": "2025-08-20T10:00:00Z"
}

Public Verification

GET https://verify.sit.i3technologies.co.ke/api/v1/credentials/verify/SIT-CRED-a9f3e2c1b0d847f6

--- Response 200 ---
{
  "valid": true,
  "learner_name": "Amina Wanjiku Ochieng",
  "course": "Electrical Installation Works",
  "level": "Certificate III",
  "grade": "PASS",
  "issued_at": "2025-08-20T10:00:00Z",
  "cert_hash": "sha256:e3b0c44298fc1c149afbf4c8996fb92427ae41e4649b934ca495991b7852b855"
}
The cert_hash is computed as SHA-256(learner_id + course_id + issue_date + grade). The hash is also written to the talent-api via an internal service-to-service call, making the credential visible on the talent platform without any additional action.

10. SIT Tutor Agent

The SIT Tutor Agent is a multilingual AI assistant deployed as a separate service at tutor.sit.i3technologies.co.ke. It is powered by LiteLLM proxying Mistral models and supports English, Swahili, and Sheng.

Capabilities

  • Course content explanation and Q&A
  • Exam preparation, practice questions, and past paper walkthrough
  • Vocational and career guidance
  • Multilingual responses (auto-detects user language)

Health Check

GET https://tutor.sit.i3technologies.co.ke/health

--- Response 200 ---
{
  "status": "ok",
  "model": "mistral-7b-instruct",
  "litellm_version": "1.43.2",
  "uptime_seconds": 84302
}

Configuration

ParameterValue / Location
LiteLLM proxy URLLITELLM_BASE_URL env var on tutor pod
Modelmistral-7b-instruct (configurable via TUTOR_MODEL)
System prompt path/app/prompts/sit_tutor_system.md inside pod
Max tokens2048 (configurable via TUTOR_MAX_TOKENS)
Supported languagesEnglish, Swahili, Sheng (auto-detect)

Common Admin DB Queries

-- Count enrollments by course
SELECT c.title, COUNT(e.id) AS total_enrollments, COUNT(cr.id) AS credentials_issued
FROM courses c
LEFT JOIN enrollments e ON e.course_id = c.id
LEFT JOIN credentials cr ON cr.course_id = c.id
GROUP BY c.id ORDER BY total_enrollments DESC;

-- Active co-working members
SELECT tier, COUNT(*) FROM membership_subscriptions
WHERE status='active' GROUP BY tier;

-- Recent eCitizen cases
SELECT case_id, service_type, status, created_at
FROM ecitizen_cases ORDER BY created_at DESC LIMIT 20;

User Guide

1. Getting Registered

To use SIT Digital Work-Centers — whether for TVET courses, eCitizen services, or booking a professional — you must first create an account. Registration is quick and requires the following information:

What You NeedWhy It Is Required
Kenya National ID or Alien Certificate NumberVerifies your identity for certification and government services
Mobile phone number (Kenyan format)SMS notifications for exam dates, membership, and service case updates
Email addressAccount login, credential delivery, and booking confirmations
Your ward (sub-county administrative ward)Required for regional service analytics and eligibility checks
Data protection consentRequired under the Kenya Data Protection Act 2019. You must agree before data is stored.

How to Register

  1. Visit sit.i3technologies.co.ke and click "Create Account".
  2. You will be redirected to the i3 SSO login page (sso.i3technologies.co.ke). Click "Register".
  3. Fill in your name, email, and password. Verify your email address via the confirmation link sent to your inbox.
  4. Return to the portal. On first login, you will be prompted to complete your learner profile (ID number, phone, ward).
  5. Read and accept the Data Protection consent notice. You cannot proceed without this step.
  6. Your account is now active and you can access all four engines.
Note: Your Kenya ID number is only used for identity verification. It is never shared with third parties and is stored securely in compliance with the Kenya Data Protection Act 2019.

2. Enrolling in a Course

  1. Log in to the portal and navigate to TVET Courses.
  2. Browse the course catalogue. Courses show the level (Certificate I–III, Diploma), duration in weeks, and a short description.
  3. Click on a course to view full details including the syllabus, assessment structure, and exam centre options.
  4. Click "Enroll Now". The system will confirm your enrollment and send a confirmation email.
  5. Your course materials will be available immediately in the My Courses section.
  6. The AI Tutor becomes available for the enrolled course automatically — see Section 9.
Tip: Check that the course has an active status before trying to enroll. Inactive courses are not available for new enrollments.

3. Booking & Taking an Exam

Booking Your Exam

  1. Once enrolled, navigate to My Courses → [Course Name] → Book Exam.
  2. Select your preferred exam date and the exam centre closest to you.
  3. Confirm the booking. You will receive an SMS and email with your EvalOS booking reference and exam centre address.
  4. Your exam booking appears under My Exams with the status confirmed.

On Exam Day

  • Bring your original Kenya National ID — no photocopies accepted.
  • Bring your EvalOS booking reference (printed or on your phone).
  • Arrive at least 30 minutes before your scheduled time.
  • Exams are conducted on-site at SIT exam centres using the EvalOS digital assessment system.
Important: Exam rescheduling must be done at least 48 hours before the exam date. Contact the centre directly or email exams@sit.i3technologies.co.ke.

4. Your Certificate & QR Code

After passing your exam, your certificate is issued automatically within 2 business days. You will receive an email notification when your credential is ready.

Accessing Your Certificate

  1. Log in and go to My Credentials.
  2. Click "Download Certificate" to get your PDF certificate.
  3. Your certificate includes a unique QR code that can be scanned by any employer or institution to verify your qualification in real time.

What Is on the Certificate?

  • Your full name (as registered)
  • Course title and TVET level
  • Date of issue
  • Grade achieved
  • A SHA-256 cryptographic hash ensuring the certificate has not been tampered with
  • QR code linking to the public verification page

5. Verifying a Credential

Anyone — employers, institutions, or government offices — can verify a SIT credential instantly without creating an account.

How to Verify

  1. Scan the QR code on the certificate using any smartphone camera or QR reader app.
  2. Or visit directly: https://verify.sit.i3technologies.co.ke/api/v1/credentials/verify/{qr_token}
  3. The verification page shows the learner's name, course, grade, issue date, and cryptographic hash.
