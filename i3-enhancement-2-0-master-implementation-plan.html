i3 Enhancement 2.0 — Master Implementation Plan

Comprehensive Upgrade Roadmap · Engineered on the i3-ai Platform · IBM ROKS Frankfurt + Nairobi
Prepared by: Solutions Architecture Review Source Folder: i3-enhancement2.0 (22 documents) Date: September 2026 Status: Implementation-Ready Draft

Strategic Premise: The i3-enhancement2.0 folder contains 22 documents spanning six distinct but deeply interconnected product streams: (1) AfriqAI sovereign frontier AI, (2) i3 AI Lab P0 infrastructure upgrades, (3) PMaaS 6.0 Gen-Z civic platform, (4) i3 EduBridge academic platform, (5) i3 SmartLabs multi-vendor lab commercialization, and (6) Enterprise Email, Collaboration & Web Hosting. Every stream builds on the same IBM ROKS OpenShift cluster already live in Frankfurt, and the same Keycloak → LiteLLM → Langfuse → OpenBao trust spine that is running today.

Core Upgrade Philosophy: Nothing here requires new vendors or unproven technology. The mandate is to wire existing namespaces together in new configurations, add GPU inference, establish metering & billing, extend to Nairobi for data residency, harden multi-tenancy, and layer language-sovereign AI (Swahili, Sheng, Gĩkũyũ) across every product surface. Then commercialise.

Combined Revenue Target: KES 730M+ Year 1 → KES 3.2B+ Year 3 across all streams, with 55–72% EBITDA margins leveraging IBM Cloud credits and partner NFR licenses as zero-cash infrastructure.
