1. What is Engage?

Engage is i3's AI-powered campaign management platform for email, WhatsApp, and SMS communications. It allows marketing teams to create contact lists, design campaigns, and deliver personalised messages at scale — with built-in consent management and AI personalisation powered by the Qwen language model.

FeatureDescription
Campaign ManagementCreate, schedule, and send email/SMS/WhatsApp campaigns
Contact ManagementImport and manage contact lists with segmentation
AI PersonalisationAutomatically personalise message body per recipient using AI
Consent GatingMessages only sent to contacts who have given valid consent
Campaign SchedulingSchedule campaigns for immediate or future delivery
Delivery AnalyticsTrack send success, opens, and failures per campaign

3. Managing Contacts

Adding Contacts

1

Navigate to Contacts in the left sidebar.

2

Click Add Contact (or Import CSV for bulk uploads).

3

Fill in: First Name, Last Name, Email Address, Phone Number, and any custom fields relevant to your organisation.

4

Set the Consent Status field — contacts must have consent: granted to receive messages via any channel.

5

Click Save. The contact is now available for campaign targeting.

Consent is mandatory: The system automatically blocks sending to any contact whose consent record is not granted. Ensure all contacts have valid consent before creating campaigns. This is enforced by the platform — it cannot be overridden.

Importing Contacts from CSV

  1. Go to Contacts → Import.
  2. Download the CSV template from the import page.
  3. Fill in the template: first_name, last_name, email, phone, consent_channel, consent_status.
  4. Upload the completed CSV file.
  5. Review the import preview — check for validation errors.
  6. Click Confirm Import.

4. Creating a Campaign

1

Go to Campaigns

Click Campaigns in the left sidebar, then click New Campaign.

2

Enter Campaign Details

Fill in:

  • Campaign Name — internal reference name
  • Subject Line — email subject (for email campaigns)
  • Channel — select Email, SMS, or WhatsApp
  • From Name / From Email — sender identity
3

Write Your Message

Enter the message body. You can use personalisation tokens:

  • {{first_name}} — replaced with each contact's first name
  • {{organisation}} — contact's organisation name
  • Enable AI Personalisation toggle to let the AI enhance each message per recipient automatically.
4

Select Contact List

Choose one or more contact lists or apply segment filters (e.g. by location, tag, or custom field).

5

Set Delivery Schedule

Choose Send Immediately or set a Scheduled Date and Time (EAT timezone). The system will queue the campaign and deliver at the specified time.

6

Review & Send

Review the campaign summary: recipient count, consent-validated count, message preview. Click Send Campaign (or Schedule if a future time was set).

AI Personalisation: When enabled, the AI generates a unique introductory paragraph for each recipient based on their name and profile. This typically improves open and engagement rates. The core message you wrote is always preserved.

5. Campaign Delivery Flow

Sync path (small campaigns):

Select contacts → Check consent per contact → AI personalise → Send via email/SMS → Log result → Update campaign status

Async path (large campaigns, >500 contacts):

Campaign queued as a CloudEvent → Kafka consumer picks up → Consent check → AI personalise in batches → Send → Log per-contact result

For large campaigns, you will see a Processing status indicator while the campaign is being dispatched. Results are updated in real time in the campaign report view.

6. Viewing Campaign Results

1

Go to Campaigns and click on any sent campaign to open its report.

2

The report shows: Total Recipients, Delivered, Failed, Bounced, Consent Blocked counts.

3

4

Use the Export button to download results as CSV for external reporting.

7. Managing Consent Records

Engage enforces consent-gated sending. No message can be sent to a contact without a valid consent record for the target channel.

Consent Channels

ChannelRequired Consent Type
Emailemail consent granted
WhatsAppwhatsapp consent granted
SMSsms consent granted

Updating a Contact's Consent

  1. Go to Contacts → search for the contact.
  2. Open the contact record.
  3. In the Consent section, update the channel consent status.
  4. Save the record. The change takes effect immediately on the next campaign run.
Unsubscribe handling: If a contact replies with "STOP" or clicks an unsubscribe link, their consent is automatically revoked for that channel. Never manually re-add consent for an unsubscribed contact without their explicit re-opt-in.
