1. What is the Admissions Agent?

The i3 Admissions Agent is an AI-powered chatbot that answers questions about academic programmes, admission requirements, fees, timetables, and registration processes. It uses Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) to search the institution's official knowledge base in real time and provides accurate, up-to-date answers — either through instant chat responses or live streaming text.

FeatureDescription
Instant Q&AAsk any admissions question and receive a direct, sourced answer

2. Accessing the Admissions Agent

1

Open the Admissions Portal

Navigate to your institution's admissions portal URL (provided by your institution, e.g. https://admissions.i3technologies.co.ke).

2

Log In with Keycloak

Click Sign In. You will be redirected to the i3 identity portal. Enter your username and password. First-time users should use the credentials sent to their email and set a new password on first login.

3

Enter the Chat Interface

After login, you will see the chat window. The AI agent is ready to answer questions immediately.

Tip: You do not need an account to browse the public admissions pages, but an account is required to use the AI chat assistant and save your conversation history.

3. Starting a Conversation

4. Using the Streaming Chat (Real-Time Responses)

If your institution has enabled the WebSocket streaming mode, you will see the agent's response appear in real time — word by word — rather than waiting for the complete answer to load. This gives a more natural conversational experience and is especially useful for longer, detailed answers.

  • Streaming starts automatically when you send a message.
  • A blinking cursor at the end of the response indicates the agent is still generating.
  • You can ask a follow-up question once the cursor disappears.
Note: If the streaming connection is interrupted (e.g. poor internet), the response may stop mid-sentence. Simply refresh the page and ask your question again.

5. Understanding Agent Responses

What the agent can answer

  • Programme listings, descriptions, and course structures
  • Entry requirements and qualification criteria
  • Fee structures and payment methods
  • Application deadlines and intake calendars
  • Campus facilities and contact information
  • Scholarship and bursary information

What the agent cannot do

  • Process or submit your application on your behalf
  • Access your personal academic records or previous applications
  • Make binding admissions decisions
  • Provide information not in the official admissions knowledge base
When in doubt: If the agent says it cannot find information, it will suggest contacting the admissions office directly. Always verify fee amounts and deadlines with the official admissions team before making financial decisions.

6. Privacy & Safety

  • All conversations are protected by your institution's tenant boundary — your data is never shared with other institutions on the platform.
  • Do not share passwords, National ID numbers, or bank details in the chat.
  • The agent includes a prompt-injection firewall — attempts to manipulate the AI into behaving improperly are automatically blocked and logged.
  • Conversation logs may be reviewed by admissions staff for quality assurance purposes.
