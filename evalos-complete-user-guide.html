1. What is EvalOS?

EvalOS is the i3 Technologies AI-powered practice examination platform designed to prepare candidates for the IBM Certified Associate Developer – watsonx Orchestrate (C1000-207) certification exam. It provides:

📋

7 Practice Sets

60 timed questions per set, mapped to all 7 IBM C1000-207 exam domains — from Platform Architecture to Deployment & Resiliency.

🤖

AI Study Coach

After each exam, an on-cluster LLM analyses your wrong answers and generates a personalised 3-day study plan.

🏅

PDF Certificates

Downloadable, verifiable completion certificates for every passing attempt — shareable with employers and LinkedIn.

🔒

Integrity Engine

Real-time anti-cheat monitoring: fullscreen enforcement, tab-switch detection, clipboard blocking, and keystroke entropy analysis.

🎤

AI Interview Round

5 questions (3 technical + 2 coding) evaluated live by AI against IBM rubrics — simulates a real technical interview.

💻

Coding Labs

Monaco editor with JS, Python, and SQL exercises focused on IBM watsonx integration — includes live AI code review.

EvalOS is deployed at evalos.i3technologies.co.ke on OpenShift, backed by Keycloak SSO and a Crunchy Postgres cluster.

5. Starting an Exam

1
Check prerequisites Each set requires the previous set to be passed (not just attempted). The card will show a 🔒 lock if the prerequisite is not met.
2
Click Start Exam (or Retake) Click the dark button on the exam card. The server will create an attempt record and load your question set.
3
Allow fullscreen EvalOS will request fullscreen mode automatically. Allow it — exiting fullscreen is logged as an integrity event. On macOS, grant fullscreen permission if prompted.
4
Exam loads The exam interface appears with a countdown timer (90:00), a progress bar, and Question 1 of 60.
Important — Attempt is counted on start Once you click Start Exam and the server creates your attempt, that counts as one attempt. If you close the browser tab, the attempt is saved and you can resume it — but the timer is still counting.

Answering Questions

  • Single choice (MC): Click a radio button. Your answer is saved automatically after each selection.
  • Multiple response (MR): Check all boxes that apply. You must get ALL correct — partial credit is not given.
  • You can change your answer at any time before submitting.
  • Unanswered questions count as incorrect.
Tip Your answers are saved to the server after each selection. If your connection drops, your progress is preserved. Reload the page — the attempt will resume automatically.

What NOT to do during an exam

  • Do not switch tabs or open another window — tab switches are logged.
  • Do not exit fullscreen — exits are counted against your trust score.
  • Do not copy or paste — clipboard events are blocked and logged.
  • Do not right-click (context menu is disabled).
  • Do not close the browser — your attempt stays in-progress but the timer keeps running.

Retake Window Enforcement (SET7)

  • Your first attempt starts the 48-hour retake window clock.
  • If you fail, you have until the deadline to start your next attempt.
  • If the deadline passes before you start attempt 2 or 3, the window is closed and the exam is locked.
  • The Dashboard shows a countdown badge: Retake deadline: DD/MM, HH:MM (48h window).
Warning The retake window enforcement is server-side. Do not rely on the browser clock — the server checks the timestamp of your first attempt at the moment you press Start.

9. AI Study Coach

The AI Study Coach analyses your exam performance and generates a personalised 300–400 word coaching report with:

  • Overall Assessment — Two sentences summarising your performance.
  • Domain Focus Areas — Two actionable tips per weak domain, referencing specific IBM watsonx Orchestrate features.
  • Key Misconceptions — 2–3 patterns identified from your wrong answers.
  • 3-Day Study Plan — Concrete daily tasks to close your knowledge gaps before the next attempt.
  • Encouragement — A personalised closing sentence.

How to Access

1
Go to your Results page after submitting any exam.
2
Click "Generate Study Coaching Report" in the AI Study Coach panel.
3
Wait 15–30 seconds — the on-cluster LLM (qwen-fast via LiteLLM) generates your report.
4
Read and act on it — copy or screenshot the report for offline study.
Tip The study coach focuses on your 3 weakest domains and up to 5 wrong answers. It's most useful when you're close to the pass threshold. If you score <50%, focus on the IBM documentation for Domain 2 (Agent & Assistant Integration) first — it's the highest-weighted domain.

After passing any exam set, EvalOS generates a verifiable PDF certificate containing:

  • Your full name and email address
  • Exam code and full title
  • Percentage score achieved
  • Issue date
  • Unique 16-character verify code
  • i3 Technologies branding

Issuing Your Certificate

1
On the Results page (after a passing attempt), click Download Certificate.
2
EvalOS issues a unique verify code and generates the PDF. This happens automatically — no manual admin step required.
3
A PDF file named i3-evalos-certificate-[VERIFYCODE].pdf is downloaded to your device.

Verifying a Certificate

Anyone can verify a certificate by visiting https://evalos.i3technologies.co.ke/verify/[VERIFYCODE] — no login required. The verification page confirms the student name, exam, score, and issue date.

Tip Re-downloading: If you need the PDF again, navigate to your Dashboard, find the attempt in Recent Attempts, click Get Certificate, and download again. The same verify code is reused (idempotent).

15. Anti-Cheat & Integrity Engine

EvalOS runs a real-time integrity monitoring system during every exam. Understanding it helps you avoid accidentally triggering integrity flags.

Events Monitored

EventHow it's detectedImpact
Focus lostWindow blur event (clicking outside the browser)-5 pts from Behavior Score per occurrence (max -25)
Tab switchdocument.visibilitychange (tab becomes hidden)-6 pts per switch (max -30)
Fullscreen exitfullscreenchange event-8 pts per exit (max -20)
Clipboard copy/pastecopy/paste events (blocked by default)-10 pts from Integrity Confidence per event (max -40)
Device fingerprint changeFingerprintJS on resume-35 pts from Identity Score
Low keystroke entropyKeystroke pattern analysis-8 to -15 pts from Behavior Score

Trust Score Calculation

Trust Score = Identity (30%) + Behavior (40%) + Integrity Confidence (30%)

If your Trust Score falls below 60, your attempt is flagged as requires_review = true and queued for human review by an administrator. Your exam result is not automatically cancelled — a human must review and make that decision.

Best practice Take your exam in a quiet environment, on a stable device, with fullscreen mode active and no other applications running. This will maximise your trust score and ensure your results are never questioned.

17. Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Do I need to answer all 60 questions?

No, but unanswered questions count as incorrect. The best strategy is to answer all questions — even a guess is better than leaving it blank. You can mark questions by navigating back using the dots.

Q: Can I pause an exam?

There is no formal pause. If you close the browser, your attempt remains in-progress and the timer continues running. When you return, the exam resumes from where you left off with the remaining time. Do not close the tab during an exam unless it's an emergency.

Q: Are the question options shuffled each time?

Yes. For Sets 1–6, options within each question are shuffled using a cryptographically secure Fisher-Yates algorithm. For SET7, both the questions themselves and their options are re-drawn and shuffled on every attempt.

Q: Will passing SET6 automatically enrol me in the programme?

Yes. Passing SET6 (≥68%) automatically triggers an enrolment record in the i3 Admissions system and dispatches an ASSESSMENT_COMPLETED event to the Admissions service. Your admissions coordinator will be notified. This is fully automated.

Q: My certificate verify code — is it case-sensitive?

Yes. The verify code is an uppercase 16-character alphanumeric string (e.g., A3F9BC14E7D20581). Enter it exactly as shown on your certificate. The verification URL is: evalos.i3technologies.co.ke/verify/[CODE]

Q: Can I share my certificate on LinkedIn?

Yes. Download the PDF and upload it as a Licence & Certification on LinkedIn. Include the verify URL in the credential URL field so recruiters can confirm it directly.

Q: Why does SET7 have a 90% pass threshold?

SET7 is the final certification-readiness assessment. It mirrors the rigour of the actual IBM C1000-207 exam, which requires a high standard of mastery across all 7 domains. The 90% threshold ensures candidates are truly exam-ready before sitting the live IBM exam.

Q: I got flagged for integrity review — does that mean I failed?

No. An integrity flag (requires_review) means an administrator will manually review your attempt. Your score stands unless the administrator determines a policy violation. You will be contacted if action is taken.

Q: Can I access EvalOS on a mobile device?

EvalOS is designed for desktop/laptop use. While the interface is responsive, fullscreen enforcement and the Monaco code editor in Coding Labs do not work well on mobile browsers. Recommended: Chrome or Edge on a Windows/macOS laptop.
