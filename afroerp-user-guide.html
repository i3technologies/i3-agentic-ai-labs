1 — Overview

AfroERP is a fully localised enterprise resource planning system built on ERPNext v15, extended with Kenya-specific modules for M-Pesa payment collection (Safaricom Daraja API) and KRA eTIMS electronic tax invoicing compliance. It handles accounting, sales, procurement, inventory, HR, and payroll in a single integrated platform.

ModuleDescription
AccountingChart of accounts, journal entries, financial statements (P&L, Balance Sheet)
Sales & InvoicingQuotations, sales orders, invoices, and credit notes
M-Pesa PaymentsSTK Push collection, B2C disbursements, automatic ledger posting
KRA eTIMSE-invoice generation and submission to KRA in compliance with Kenya tax law
InventoryWarehousing, stock transfers, purchase orders, and supplier management
HR & PayrollEmployee records, leave management, payroll runs, P9 forms (Kenya PAYE)
Mfumo Co-workerAI assistant for financial analysis, compliance queries, and report drafting

2 — Login & Initial Setup

  1. Navigate to https://afroerp.i3technologies.co.ke.
  2. Click Login with SSO and authenticate via the i3 portal.
  3. On first login, the Setup Wizard runs — complete your Company Name, Country (Kenya), Currency (KES), and Financial Year start date.
  4. Upload your company logo and configure the default bank account.
NoteERPNext uses role-based access. Your administrator must assign you a role (Accounts Manager, Sales User, Stock Manager, etc.) before you can access specific modules.

3 — Accounting

3.1 Chart of Accounts

AfroERP ships with a Kenya-localised Chart of Accounts pre-loaded. Navigate to Accounting → Chart of Accounts to review or add accounts. Standard categories follow KRA's prescribed structure for VAT and corporate tax returns.

3.2 Journal Entries

  1. Go to Accounting → Journal Entry → New.
  2. Select the entry type (Journal Entry, Debit Note, Credit Note, etc.).
  3. Add debit and credit rows — each must reference an account from the Chart of Accounts.
  4. Total debits must equal total credits. Click Submit to post.

3.3 Financial Statements

Go to Accounting → Financial Statements and select Profit and Loss Statement, Balance Sheet, or Cash Flow Statement. Filter by date range, company, and cost centre. Export as PDF or Excel.

4 — Sales & Invoicing

4.1 Raising a Sales Invoice

  1. Go to Selling → Sales Invoice → New.
  2. Select the Customer. If they don't exist, click Create New Customer and enter name, PIN, and KRA PIN.
  3. Add line items: select items from the Item master, enter quantity and rate. VAT (16%) is calculated automatically if the item is taxable.
  4. Click Save to review, then Submit to post. The invoice is now payable.
  5. If eTIMS is enabled, the invoice is automatically submitted to KRA on submission (see Section 6).

4.2 Credit Notes

To reverse a posted invoice, open the invoice and click Create → Return / Credit Note. Enter the reason and click Submit. The credit note posts and reverses the ledger entries.

5 — M-Pesa Payments

5.1 Collecting Payment via STK Push

  1. Open a submitted Sales Invoice. Click Payment → M-Pesa STK Push.
  2. Confirm or enter the customer's phone number (2547XXXXXXXX) and the invoice amount.
  3. Click Send STK Push. The customer receives a M-Pesa payment prompt.
  4. Once the customer enters their PIN, the payment is confirmed and a Payment Entry is automatically created and reconciled against the invoice.

5.2 Paying Suppliers via B2C

  1. Go to Accounts Payable → Payment Entry → New.
  2. Set Payment Type to Pay, Party Type to Supplier, and select the supplier.
  3. Select payment mode M-Pesa B2C and enter the supplier's phone number.
  4. Click Submit. The disbursement is sent via Daraja API and the ledger is updated.
WarningM-Pesa transactions are irreversible once confirmed. Verify all amounts and phone numbers before submitting.

6 — KRA eTIMS Integration

AfroERP's eTIMS module automatically submits electronic tax invoices to the Kenya Revenue Authority in real time, as required by the Tax Procedures Act (Digital Services).

Document TypeKRA Submission
Sales Invoice (VAT applicable)Auto-submitted on Submit. CU Invoice Number returned from KRA and printed on invoice PDF.
Credit NoteAuto-submitted as a credit to KRA. References original CU Invoice Number.
Stock movement (applicable items)Goods declaration submitted on warehouse release.
NoteeTIMS submission requires your company's eTIMS Device Serial Number and PIN to be configured in AfroERP Settings → KRA eTIMS by your system administrator.

Checking Submission Status

On any Sales Invoice, click the eTIMS tab to see submission status (Pending, Submitted, Failed), the KRA CU Invoice Number, and a link to the signed e-invoice XML.

7 — Inventory Management

7.1 Creating Items

  1. Go to Stock → Item → New.
  2. Enter item code, name, item group, and unit of measure.
  3. Under Tax, set the KRA HS Code and whether VAT applies (Standard 16% or Zero-rated).
  4. Click Save. The item is now available in invoices and purchase orders.

7.2 Purchase Orders & Stock Receipts

  1. Go to Buying → Purchase Order → New. Select supplier and add items.
  2. Submit the PO. When goods arrive, open the PO and click Create → Purchase Receipt.
  3. Confirm received quantities and click Submit. Stock levels update automatically.

8 — HR & Payroll

8.1 Employee Setup

  1. Go to HR → Employee → New. Enter personal details, Department, Designation, and KRA PIN.
  2. Set the Salary Structure (Basic, House Allowance, Transport). Configure NHIF, NSSF, and PAYE deductions.
  3. Save and submit the employee record.

8.2 Running Payroll

  1. Go to Payroll → Payroll Entry → New.
  2. Select the frequency (Monthly), date range, and department.
  3. Click Get Employees then Create Salary Slips. Review individual slips if needed.
  4. Click Submit to finalise. Optionally click Bank File to export for bank bulk payment.
NoteP9 forms (annual PAYE certificate) for KRA filing are generated under Payroll → P9 Report. Export as PDF per employee or as a CSV for iTax bulk upload.

10 — Troubleshooting

IssueFix
eTIMS submission failedCheck the eTIMS Status tab on the invoice. Common error: Invalid PIN — verify the customer KRA PIN in the Customer master. Retry using Resubmit to KRA.
M-Pesa STK Push not receivedVerify the phone number format (2547XXXXXXXX). Ensure the customer has sufficient M-Pesa float. Check AfroERP → M-Pesa Logs for the error code.
Salary slip not generatingEnsure the employee has a valid Salary Structure assigned and the employment start date falls within the payroll period.
Stock balance negative after receiptOpen the Purchase Receipt and verify the target warehouse is correctly set. Check Stock → Stock Ledger to trace the discrepancy.
Cannot submit invoice (validation error)Mandatory fields missing — check Customer KRA PIN, item HS Code, and that all accounts in the invoice exist in the Chart of Accounts.
