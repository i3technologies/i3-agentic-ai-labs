1 — Overview

PMaaS (Political Management as a Service) is a comprehensive campaign operations platform designed for Kenyan political campaigns. It combines voter data management, mass communication, AI-powered strategic briefings, and M-Pesa payment processing in a single, secure platform.

FeatureDescription
Campaign ManagementCreate and track multiple concurrent campaigns with milestones and budgets
AI BriefingAI-generated strategic briefs based on voter data, demographics, and issue tracking
Voter DatabaseImport, segment, and manage voter records by ward, constituency, and county
M-Pesa IntegrationCollect harambee contributions and pay field agents via Safaricom Daraja API
Dawa Co-workerAI assistant for campaign strategy, speech drafting, and issue analysis

2 — Login & Account Setup

  1. Navigate to https://pmaas.i3technologies.co.ke.
  2. Click Sign In — you are redirected to the i3 SSO portal.
  3. Enter your @i3technologies.co.ke email and password, then click Log In.
  4. On first login, complete your profile: name, role (Campaign Manager / Field Coordinator / Finance Officer), and region.
NoteYour role determines which modules you can access. Campaign Managers have full access; Field Coordinators see voter data and scheduling only; Finance Officers access the M-Pesa module.

4 — Managing Campaigns

4.1 Creating a Campaign

  1. Click Campaigns in the sidebar, then New Campaign.
  2. Fill in campaign name, election type (General / By-election / Party Primary), target seat, and election date.
  3. Set a campaign budget and assign a Campaign Manager.
  4. Click Create. The campaign workspace opens with Milestones, Voter Map, and Communications tabs.

4.2 Adding Milestones

  1. Inside the campaign, click the Milestones tab.
  2. Click Add Milestone. Enter name, due date, responsible person, and success criteria.
  3. Mark milestones complete by clicking the checkbox when done. Progress updates the campaign progress bar on the Dashboard.

5 — AI Campaign Briefing

The AI Briefing feature uses the on-cluster Qwen 14B model to analyse your campaign data and generate a strategic brief covering voter sentiment, key issues, and recommended talking points.

  1. Open a campaign and click the AI Brief tab.
  2. Select the brief type: Weekly Strategy, Event Preparation, or Opposition Analysis.
  3. Optionally add context: paste in recent news articles, survey data, or specific questions.
  4. Click Generate Brief. The AI processes your campaign data and returns a structured brief (usually within 30–60 seconds).
  5. Review the brief. Use Export PDF to save or Share to send to team members.
NoteAI briefs are generated entirely on-cluster. No campaign data is sent to external services.

6 — Voter & Constituency Management

6.1 Importing Voter Data

  1. Go to Voters in the sidebar, then click Import.
  2. Download the CSV template and populate it with voter records (ID number, name, ward, polling station, phone).
  3. Upload the completed CSV. The system validates and deduplicates records.

6.2 Segmenting Voters

Use the Filter panel to slice the voter database by ward, age group, gender, or contact status. Saved segments can be used as targets for SMS or WhatsApp campaigns via the Engage integration.

7 — M-Pesa Payments

PMaaS integrates with the Safaricom Daraja API for both inbound collections (harambee, contributions) and outbound disbursements (field agent allowances).

7.1 Collecting Contributions (STK Push)

  1. Go to Payments → Collect.
  2. Enter the contributor's phone number (format: 2547XXXXXXXX), amount, and reference.
  3. Click Send STK Push. The contributor receives a prompt on their phone to enter their M-Pesa PIN.
  4. Status updates to Confirmed once payment completes. The transaction appears in Payments history.

7.2 Paying Field Agents (B2C)

  1. Go to Payments → Disburse.
  2. Select agents from your Field Staff list or upload a CSV with phone numbers and amounts.
  3. Add a narration (e.g. "Allowance – Nairobi Rally 12 Jul") and click Confirm Disbursement.
  4. Each agent receives a Safaricom SMS confirmation. Transactions are logged with timestamps.
WarningDisbursements are irreversible once confirmed. Double-check all phone numbers and amounts before proceeding.
