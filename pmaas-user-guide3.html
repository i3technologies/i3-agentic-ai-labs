1. What is PMaaS?

PMaaS (Political Marketing as a Service) is an AI-powered platform designed for Kenyan political campaigns, particularly ward-level and county-level campaigns. It provides daily strategy briefings, voter analysis by ward, manifesto question answering, and automated generation of campaign content for WhatsApp, social media, and voice messages.

Daily Briefings Ward Analysis Manifesto Q&A WhatsApp Content Social Media Posts Voice Scripts
Important: PMaaS is a Supervised Draft / L1 AI system. All AI-generated content is a draft proposal. A human campaign manager must review and approve every piece of content before it is used publicly. The AI assists — it does not decide.

3. Daily Campaign Briefing

Each morning, PMaaS generates a personalised daily briefing for your campaign — a summary of strategic priorities, voter sentiment insights, and recommended focus areas for the day.

1

Access the Briefing

From the dashboard, click Today's Briefing. Or navigate to Briefing in the top menu.

2

Review the Briefing Content

The briefing includes:

  • Priority wards to focus on today
  • Key manifesto talking points most relevant to today's context
  • Recommended outreach channels (WhatsApp / voice / SMS)
  • Suggested content themes based on recent engagement data

3

Ask Follow-Up Questions

Use the Ask field at the bottom of the briefing to ask clarifying questions. Example: "Which ward has the lowest youth voter registration in Machakos East?" The AI will answer from the manifesto and voter database.

4. Manifesto Q&A

Ask the AI questions about your candidate's manifesto, policy positions, or any registered commitment — and get instant, accurate, sourced answers.

1

Open Ask Manifesto

Click Ask in the navigation bar.

2

Type Your Question

Ask in plain English or Swahili. Examples:

  • "What does the manifesto say about water access in rural wards?"
  • "What is our position on county bursary funds?"
  • "Je, msimamo wa mgombea ni nini kuhusu elimu ya awali?"

3

Review the Answer

The AI provides a precise answer with references to the relevant manifesto section. Use this to prepare spokespersons for media or public engagements.

Tip: If you cannot find an answer, it may mean that topic is not covered in the uploaded manifesto. Contact your administrator to ensure the latest manifesto version has been uploaded to the system.

5. Ward-Level Voter Analysis

The Ward Analysis tool provides AI-generated insights on voter demographics, key issues, and strategic recommendations at the ward level.

1

Open Ward Analysis

Click Analyze in the navigation.

2

Select Ward(s)

Type the ward name or select from the map. You can analyse a single ward or compare multiple wards.

3

Specify Your Analysis Focus

Choose focus areas: youth voters, women voters, employment concerns, infrastructure priorities, etc. Or leave blank for a general analysis.

4

Run Analysis

Click Analyse Ward. The AI uses the Qwen Heavy model to generate a detailed strategic analysis. This may take up to 30 seconds.

5

Review & Export

The analysis includes voter segment breakdown, top issues, strategic talking points, and recommended outreach channels. Click Export PDF to download for your campaign team.

6. Generating Campaign Content

PMaaS can generate ready-to-use campaign content in multiple formats. All generated content is a draft — it must be reviewed and approved by a human before publishing or sending.

1

Open Content Generator

Click Content in the navigation.

2

Specify Content Parameters

Fill in:

  • Content Type — WhatsApp message, social media post, voice script, SMS
  • Target Audience — youth, business owners, women's groups, farmers, etc.
  • Ward / Region — localise the message
  • Theme — specific manifesto topic to address (e.g. bursaries, roads, water)
  • Tone — inspirational, informational, urgent, conversational

3

Generate Draft

Click Generate. The AI produces 2–3 draft variations of the content.

4

Review and Edit

Read each draft carefully. Edit as needed for factual accuracy, tone, and local context. The AI draft is a starting point — your team's knowledge of the ward context is essential.

5

Approve & Use

Once satisfied, click Approve to mark the content as campaign-ready. Approved content can be exported or sent directly via the Engage platform integration (if enabled).

7. Consent & Voter Privacy

  • PMaaS only sends messages to voters who have given consent for the relevant channel (WhatsApp, voice, SMS).
  • Voter identifiers (phone numbers, National IDs) are stored using HMAC-SHA256 anonymisation — they cannot be read in plain text in the system.
  • Never attempt to input raw National ID numbers into the platform.
  • All AI-assisted actions are logged in the Agent Registry for compliance and audit purposes.
Compliance: Kenyan data protection law (Data Protection Act, 2019) requires valid consent for all political messaging. Ensure your campaign's consent management records are up to date before any outreach.
