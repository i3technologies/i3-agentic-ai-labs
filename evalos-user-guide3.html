1. What is EvalOS?

EvalOS is i3's AI-powered assessment platform. It supports multiple assessment formats for academic institutions, certification bodies, and employers. Candidates can take randomised exams, get AI-powered study coaching, practice coding challenges, and attend AI-simulated job interviews.

AI InterviewSimulated job interview with AI evaluation of responses
Coding LabIn-browser coding challenges with AI code review (powered by Qwen Coder)
Digital CertificatesDownloadable verified certificate on passing an exam
Prerequisite GatingMust pass prerequisite exams (≥90%) before accessing advanced modules

2. Logging In

1

Navigate to EvalOS

Go to https://evalos.i3technologies.co.ke.

2

Sign In

Click Sign In. Enter your i3 platform email and password on the Keycloak login screen. Use "Forgot Password" if needed.

3

Access Your Dashboard

Your dashboard shows available exams, your recent attempt history, and any pending prerequisites.

3. Taking an Exam — Step by Step

1

Browse Available Exams

On the dashboard, click Exams. You will see all exams available to you. Exams with a LOCKED badge require a prerequisite — hover to see which exam to complete first.

2

Start the Exam

Click on an unlocked exam, then click Start Exam. Read the exam instructions carefully — note the time limit and number of questions. Click Begin to start the timer.

3

Answer Questions

Questions are presented one at a time or as a scrollable list depending on exam configuration. Select your answer from the options provided. Use the navigation buttons to move between questions. You can mark questions for review and return to them before submitting.

4

Submit the Exam

When you are ready, click Submit Exam. A confirmation dialog will show how many questions are answered and how many are marked for review. Click Confirm Submit to finalise.

If time runs out, the exam submits automatically with your answers as-is.

5

View Your Results

Your score is displayed immediately. A PASSED badge appears if you met or exceeded the pass threshold (default: 90%). You can review which questions you got wrong and see the correct answers with explanations.

Attempt limits: Each exam has a maximum number of attempts (default: 5). Once exhausted, contact your administrator to request a reset. Attempt counts and scores are permanently recorded.

Resuming an Incomplete Exam

If your session is interrupted (e.g. browser crash), EvalOS saves your progress automatically. Return to the exam on the dashboard and click Resume. Your previous answers and remaining time will be restored.

4. Exam Security (Anti-Cheat)

EvalOS includes an anti-cheat system to maintain exam integrity:

  • Tab switching detection — switching to another browser tab is logged.
  • Focus loss events — if the exam window loses focus, it is recorded.
  • Random question order — questions and answer options are shuffled uniquely per attempt.
  • Copy-paste restriction — right-click and copy functions may be disabled for certain exams.
Important: Anti-cheat events are reviewed by evaluators. Multiple tab-switch events may result in your attempt being flagged for review. Take exams in a distraction-free environment.

5. Study Coach (AI Learning Assistant)

After completing an exam attempt — whether you pass or fail — the Study Coach is available to help you improve.

1

Open Study Coach

From your attempt result page, click Study Coach. Alternatively, go to Dashboard → Attempts → select attempt → Study Coach.

2

Review Your Analysis

The Study Coach analyses your answers and identifies weak areas. It provides a personalised study plan with specific topics to revisit.

3

Ask Follow-Up Questions

Use the chat interface to ask the Study Coach to explain any concept you found difficult. Example: "Explain why Option B was the correct answer for question 7."

4

Follow the Study Plan

The Study Coach generates a prioritised list of topics. Work through the resources before your next attempt.

6. AI Interview Practice

The AI Interview feature simulates a real job interview with an AI evaluator. Ideal for candidates preparing for technical or competency-based interviews.

1

Start an AI Interview

Go to Interviews from the dashboard and select the interview type (e.g. Software Engineer, Data Analyst). Click Begin Interview.

2

Answer Each Question

The AI interviewer asks structured questions. Type your response in the text box. Take your time — there is no strict time limit per question for practice sessions.

3

Receive AI Evaluation

After the interview, the AI evaluates each of your answers for content, clarity, and relevance. You receive a score and detailed feedback for every question.

4

Review & Improve

Use the feedback to refine your answers. You can retake the interview as many times as needed.

7. Coding Lab

The Coding Lab provides in-browser coding challenges with instant AI feedback powered by the Qwen Coder model.

1

Open Coding Lab

Click Coding Lab in the navigation. Select a challenge by topic (e.g. Python, JavaScript, Algorithms, Data Structures).

2

Read the Problem

The problem statement, expected inputs/outputs, and constraints are shown on the left panel.

3

Write Your Solution

Type your code in the editor on the right. Select your programming language from the dropdown.

4

Run & Submit

Click Run Tests to execute against sample test cases. Click Submit when ready to submit your final solution for grading.

5

Get AI Code Review

Click AI Review after submission. The AI will review your code for correctness, efficiency, style, and suggest improvements.

8. Downloading Your Certificate

When you pass an exam, a digital certificate is generated automatically.

  1. Go to Dashboard → Attempts → select a passed attempt.
  2. Click Download Certificate.
  3. The certificate PDF includes your name, exam title, score, date, and a verification code.
  4. Share the verification code with employers/institutions to verify your achievement.
