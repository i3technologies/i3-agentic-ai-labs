Access Local
County Services.

Integrated Audit Management System

The IAMS streamlines documentation and audits, reduces duplication, ensures compliance, and boosts efficiency and cross-department alignment.

Machakos PSV Management System

A smart PSV management system for regulating public transport in Machakos County, enhancing safety, compliance, route management, and operational efficiency.

Open Citizen Portal

An online citizen engagement portal providing easy access to county services, information, and feedback, promoting transparency and public participation.

Online Jobs Portal

A county jobs portal offering transparent access to public sector job opportunities and a streamlined online recruitment and application process

Biashara App

A business support app that simplifies licensing, permits, and compliance for traders and MSMEs, improving ease of doing business in Machakos County.

Macha Pay

A secure digital payment platform for paying county fees, levies, and services online, ensuring fast, transparent, and efficient revenue collection.

HR Services

A human resource management system for recruitment, payroll, performance, and staff records, improving workforce efficiency and service delivery.

Tourism

A county tourism platform promoting Machakos attractions, culture, and destinations while supporting local tourism businesses and investment.
