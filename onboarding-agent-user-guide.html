1. What is the Onboarding Agent?

The i3 Onboarding Agent is an AI system that automatically generates personalised learning and onboarding plans for new platform users. Based on your role, the agent runs parallel AI scans across skill domains, synthesises the results using the Granite Heavy language model, and produces a structured plan with timeline — including a Markdown document and a visual Gantt chart.

2. Logging In

1

Access the Onboarding Portal

Your administrator or HR team will share the onboarding portal URL (hosted at i3-onboarding namespace). Navigate to it in your browser.

2

Sign In

Click Sign In. Authenticate with your i3 SSO credentials (email + password). First-time users must set a password using the link emailed to them.

3

Confirm Your Role

On first login, you will be asked to confirm your role. This determines the content of your onboarding plan.

3. Generating Your Onboarding Plan

1

Click "Generate My Onboarding Plan"

From the home screen, click the large Generate My Onboarding Plan button. The system will automatically detect your role from your SSO profile.

2

Wait for AI Processing

The agent runs three stages in parallel:

  1. Skill domain scans (ChromaDB knowledge base search per skill area)
  2. Verification pass (cross-check and quality check)
  3. Plan synthesis (Granite Heavy LLM generates your structured plan)
This typically takes 30–60 seconds. A progress indicator shows each stage completing.

3

Review Your Plan

Your personalised onboarding plan appears. It includes:

  • Week-by-week learning milestones
  • Key skills and tools to learn for your role
  • Platform modules to explore
  • Resources and documentation links
  • Estimated completion timeline

4. Viewing Your Plan

JSON View

The structured JSON data of your plan — useful for integrations or programmatic access.

Markdown View

A formatted, readable Markdown document suitable for sharing with your team lead or printing. Access via the Markdown tab in your plan view.

Gantt Chart View

A visual timeline of your onboarding milestones. Access via the Gantt tab. Shows each milestone as a bar on a timeline — weeks 1 through N, colour-coded by category (learning, practice, assessment).

Tip: Share the Gantt chart with your manager during your first week check-in. It provides an immediate visual of your progress trajectory and the milestones to hit in the first 30 days.

5. Downloading Your Plan

1

Open your plan and click the Markdown tab.

2

Click Download (.md) to save the Markdown file to your computer.

3

Open the file in any Markdown viewer (VS Code, Obsidian, Typora) or share it directly via email or messaging.

Plan expiry: Plans in the system cache expire after 2 hours. Download your Markdown file or sync to CRM before then if you want a permanent record.

6. CRM Sync (Two-Step Process)

To permanently record your onboarding plan in the CRM for progress tracking by your team lead or HR, use the two-step CRM sync:

1

Initiate Sync

From your plan view, click Sync to CRM. This is the Prepare step — it stages your plan and shows you a preview of what will be written to the CRM.

2

Review the Staged Data

Review the staged record. Confirm the plan details, your name, role, and milestones are correct.

3

Confirm Sync

Click Confirm Sync. The plan is permanently written to the CRM. Your team lead will be able to view and track your progress against it.

4

Cancel if Needed

If you spotted an error in the preview, click Cancel to discard the staged sync without writing to CRM. You can regenerate your plan and try again.

7. Checking Onboarding Status

Go to the Status page to see:

  • Your most recent plan generation status (Ready / Processing / Expired)
  • CRM sync history (Pending / Synced / Cancelled)
  • Platform connectivity status (ChromaDB availability check)
